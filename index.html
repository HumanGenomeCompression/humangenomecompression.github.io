3. SCIENTIFIC PROTOCOL

Reproducibility & methodology

01 / Reference

Reference sequence

Benchmark of lossless compression on the DNA symbol sequence derived from T2T-CHM13 v2.0 (Nurk et al., 2022). Consult the repository for exact preprocessing, replication scripts and experimental conditions.

02 / Eligibility & metrics

Ranking criterion

Only configurations with a published replication script are included. The compressor binary must not exceed 10 MB, and the decompressor binary must not exceed 1 MB. Peak RAM usage must not exceed 32 GB during either compression or decompression, and peak disk space usage must not exceed 50 GB. Configurations are ranked by exact compressed bytes. Bits per symbol and reduction are recalculated from the benchmark sequence length.

bps = 8 × compressed bytes / N
reduction (%) = 100 × (1 − bps / 2)
03 / Resource measurements

Experimental comparability

All new submissions will be independently benchmarked on the same designated Linux machine, for both compression and decompression, to ensure consistent and comparable resource measurements. Distributed execution across multiple machines is not permitted. Historical results may have been obtained on different systems and will require re-evaluation under this protocol. Missing values remain unavailable.

04 / Open benchmark

Benchmark data

Results are embedded directly in this HTML document. Eligibility requires a published replication script, a compressor binary no larger than 10 MB, a decompressor binary no larger than 1 MB, a maximum of 32 GB RAM during each operation, and a maximum of 50 GB disk space. New results can be added by updating the embedded data.

4. CONTRIBUTE

Submit a new benchmark entry

Open scientific challenge

Push the limits of genome compression.

To submit a new entry, send an email to pratas@ua.pt with the three required files. All submissions will be evaluated on the same designated Linux machine. Both executables and the replication script must run locally on that machine without relying on additional machines. Submissions must comply with the scientific protocol above, including the 10 MB compressor binary and 1 MB decompressor binary size limits, 32 GB maximum RAM for both compression and decompression, and 50 GB maximum disk space usage.

Submit by email ↗
